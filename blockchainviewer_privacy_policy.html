1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VinATech Limited ("VinATech", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Blockchain Viewer mobile application (the "App").

VinATech Limited is the controller of the personal data it processes as described in this policy. Other providers may be responsible for information they process under their own privacy policies.

The App allows users to view publicly available blockchain information, such as cryptocurrency addresses, blocks, and transactions. It does not provide cryptocurrency exchange, custody, wallet, cryptocurrency payment, investment, or financial-advice services. On first installation, the App provides a free Demo licence for a limited period, with access to all features. You can also purchase access for a limited period through Google Play. These are one-time purchases and do not renew automatically.

Before you can use the App, we show an in-app notice with links to this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use. You cannot continue unless you accept. Limited technical processing needed to start the App may occur before you make that choice.

Privacy contact: support@vinatech.it.com Website: vinatech.it.com

2. Information you provide

You can search for coins by name or symbol in the App's search field. The App matches what you type against its coin list on your device. It does not log your search text, save it as search history, or send it to VinATech Limited or another service to process the search. Opening a search result may retrieve coin data from the providers listed in Section 5.

The App does not ask for or require private keys, wallet passwords, recovery phrases, seed phrases, or exchange credentials. Never enter this information into the App.

If you contact us for support or to make a privacy request, we may process your contact details and the information you choose to provide so we can respond.

3. Information processed automatically

When the App connects to internet services, information needed to deliver requested content, manage access, and maintain security may be processed automatically. This may include:

  • your Internet Protocol (IP) address;
  • the date and time of a request;
  • app version, operating-system version, and device type;
  • network and diagnostic information;
  • a device-based identifier, a device-linked sign-in identifier, and a Support ID used to manage trial and purchased access; and
  • trial status and, if you make a purchase, the package selected, purchase time, an order reference where available, and the status and expiry of your access.

Google Play handles payment details when you buy access. We receive a purchase token (a code used to verify the purchase) and check it with Google Play before granting access. We do not receive your full payment card details.

VinATech Limited does not use this information to build advertising profiles or sell personal information. Internet service providers, hosting providers, and blockchain-data providers may process technical request data under their own privacy terms.

4. How information is used

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

  • retrieve and display publicly available blockchain and market information;
  • manage trial access, verify purchases with Google Play, and provide purchased access;
  • respond to support requests and help prevent purchases from being used more than once;
  • operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the App;
  • diagnose errors and prevent abuse; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data.

Legal bases for processing (UK and EEA)

Where UK or EEA data-protection law applies, we rely on the following legal bases for the purposes described above:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the App and requested content, manage the free Demo licence, verify purchases, and provide paid access under our Terms of Use.
  • Legitimate interests: to start and secure the App, detect errors and misuse, prevent a purchase from being used more than once, and respond to support enquiries. Our interests are in operating a reliable service, protecting users and purchases, and resolving problems, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligations: to respond to privacy requests and keep or disclose information when required by applicable law.

Accepting our Terms of Use and acknowledging this Privacy Policy is not consent to unrelated data uses. If we introduce processing that requires your consent, we will ask for it separately and explain how you can withdraw it.

6. Public blockchain data

Information recorded on a public blockchain is maintained by a decentralised network rather than by VinATech Limited. Blockchain records may be permanent and generally cannot be changed or deleted by us. We do not control the content, availability, accuracy, or retention of public blockchain data displayed through the App.

7. Data retention and deletion

Coin searches: The App searches for coins on your device. VinATech Limited does not retain the text entered in the search field.

Trial and purchase records: Demo access lasts 7 to 14 days, depending on the App version. Purchased packages provide access for up to one year. These are access periods, not data-retention periods. We keep records needed to manage an active Demo or purchased licence. After access ends, records may still be needed to resolve an outstanding support, refund, or purchase dispute, prevent a purchase from being reused, or meet a legal obligation. Expiry, cancellation, a refund, or uninstalling the App does not automatically erase these records. You can request their deletion as described below, subject to those needs and applicable law.

Technical logs: Technical and security logs, if collected, are retained under the settings of the service that generates them. The period can vary by provider and may be affected by an ongoing technical or security investigation or a legal obligation. We do not currently apply one fixed retention period to all technical logs.

On-device data: Coins you add to Favourites are saved on your device. The Favourites list is excluded from Android backup and device transfer. You can remove it, along with locally stored preferences and other App data, by clearing the App's storage through Android settings or uninstalling the App. This does not itself delete records held on our servers or by Google Play.

You do not need to create a named account to use the App, but we use a device-linked sign-in identifier to manage access. This identifier can be associated with your device, Support ID, and purchase records, so we treat it as personal data. To request access to or deletion of information held by VinATech Limited, contact support@vinatech.it.com and include your in-app Support ID if available. We may need to verify your request and retain limited information needed for purchase validation, security, legal compliance, or legal claims. We cannot delete Google Play's own purchase records or information recorded on a public blockchain.

8. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information processed through the App. Network communications containing personal or sensitive information are transmitted using secure encrypted connections, such as HTTPS, where supported by the relevant service.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users remain responsible for protecting their devices and for not entering private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or other confidential credentials into the App.
