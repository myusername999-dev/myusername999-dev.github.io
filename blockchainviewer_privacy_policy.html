1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VinATech Limited ("VinATech", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Blockchain Viewer mobile application (the "App").

The App allows users to view publicly available blockchain information, such as cryptocurrency addresses, blocks, and transactions. The App is provided for informational purposes only and does not provide cryptocurrency exchange, custody, wallet, payment, investment, or financial-advice services.

Privacy contact: support@vinatech.it.com Website: vinatech.it.com

2. Information you provide

The App may allow you to enter or search for public blockchain information, including:

  • public wallet or blockchain addresses;
  • transaction identifiers or hashes; and
  • block numbers or other public blockchain identifiers.

Blockchain records are public by design. Although a public blockchain address may not directly identify a person, it could potentially be linked to an individual when combined with other information. You should therefore avoid entering information that you do not wish to query through the App.

The App does not ask for or require private keys, wallet passwords, recovery phrases, seed phrases, or exchange credentials. Never enter this information into the App.

3. Information processed automatically

When the App connects to internet services, limited technical information may be processed automatically to deliver requested content and maintain security. This may include:

  • your Internet Protocol (IP) address;
  • the date and time of a request;
  • app version, operating-system version, and device type;
  • network and diagnostic information; and
  • requested blockchain addresses, transaction identifiers, or block identifiers.

VinATech Limited does not use this information to build advertising profiles or sell personal information. Internet service providers, hosting providers, and blockchain-data providers may process technical request data under their own privacy terms.

4. How information is used

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

  • retrieve and display publicly available blockchain information requested by the user;
  • operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the App;
  • diagnose errors and prevent abuse; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data.

6. Public blockchain data

Information recorded on a public blockchain is maintained by a decentralised network rather than by VinATech Limited. Blockchain records may be permanent and generally cannot be changed or deleted by us. We do not control the content, availability, accuracy, or retention of public blockchain data displayed through the App.

7. Data retention and deletion

App queries: VinATech Limited does not retain blockchain searches or identifiers after they are used to provide the requested result, except where temporary processing is technically necessary or limited records must be retained for security, troubleshooting, or legal compliance.

Technical logs: Technical and security logs, if collected, are retained for 30 days and then deleted or anonymised unless a longer period is required by law or necessary to investigate security incidents.

On-device data: Any locally stored preferences or recent searches can be removed by clearing the App's storage through Android settings, or uninstalling the App.

The App does not currently provide user accounts. Therefore, there is no user account to delete. If account functionality is introduced, this policy and the App will be updated to explain how users can request account and associated-data deletion.

To request deletion of personal information held directly by VinATech Limited, contact support@vinatech.it.com. We may need to retain limited information where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. We cannot delete information recorded on a public blockchain.

8. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information processed through the App. Network communications containing personal or sensitive information are transmitted using secure encrypted connections, such as HTTPS, where supported by the relevant service.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users remain responsible for protecting their devices and for not entering private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or other confidential credentials into the App.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process technical information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal data.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, and the right to complain to a competent data-protection authority.

To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, contact support@vinatech.it.com. We may need to verify your request. These rights may be limited in some circumstances under applicable law. Public blockchain information is not controlled by VinATech Limited and cannot generally be corrected or erased by us.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you may also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact support@vinatech.it.com so that we can investigate and, where appropriate, delete it.

12. Third-party links and services

The App may display information or links originating from third-party blockchain networks, explorers, websites, or services. VinATech Limited does not control those third parties. Their processing of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our service providers, legal requirements, or our information-handling practices change. We will publish the revised policy at this location and update the "Last updated" date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
