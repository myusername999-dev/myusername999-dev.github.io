Privacy And Data Protection

Privacy Policy

VINATECH Limited is committed to lawful, fair, and transparent handling of personal information. This policy explains what data we process, why we process it, and how we protect it.

1. Scope And Principles

This policy applies to VINATECH Limited websites, digital services, and business communication channels. We process personal data only where it is relevant to providing our services, supporting customers, protecting security, or complying with legal obligations.

We align our practices with key privacy principles reflected in UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, including purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, and confidentiality.

2. Data We Process

Depending on your interaction with us, we may process:

  • Identity and contact details you submit (for example name, business email, role, company).
  • Service-related communications and support records.
  • Technical event logs required for security, fault analysis, and service continuity.

No Cookies Collected

VINATECH Limited does not use analytics cookies, marketing cookies, or social media tracking cookies on this website. We do not deploy cookie banners for profiling because no such cookies are collected.

No Marketing Profiling

We do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell personal data to third parties. Where informational emails are required for active service relationships, they are limited to operational communications.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and deliver requested services.
  • To provide support and maintain platform reliability.
  • To secure systems and investigate misuse or abuse.
  • To comply with regulatory, contractual, and legal duties.

4. Enforcement And Data Protection Controls

VINATECH Limited applies a structured compliance model informed by public policy approaches from Google Play Developer Content Policy (privacy, deception prevention, responsible data handling, and clear user disclosures) and by data protection governance themes described by the Scottish Judiciary guidance on privacy and data protection.

  • Periodic policy reviews and internal audits of data processing activities.
  • Role-based access controls and least-privilege data access.
  • Documented incident response and escalation procedures.
  • Corrective action workflow for potential policy breaches, including remediation and recordkeeping.
  • Cooperation with supervisory or competent authorities where legally required.
If you believe your data has been handled in a way that is inconsistent with this policy, contact VINATECH Limited using the support channels listed on this website and we will investigate promptly.

5. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal data, correction of inaccurate data, restriction of processing, erasure where lawful, and information about how your data is used.

Requests are reviewed with attention to legal obligations, security requirements, and any applicable exemptions.

6. Retention, Security, And Updates

We retain personal information only for as long as required by legitimate business purposes or legal obligations. Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards are applied to protect confidentiality and integrity.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be published on this page.
